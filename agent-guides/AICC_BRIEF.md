## What is AI Clarity for Chiefs?

AI Clarity for Chiefs (AICC) is a four-week, one-on-one executive engagement for Chiefs and VIPs who want personal clarity on AI without the intensity of a cohort challenge. It is delivered through eight private online sessions (two per week), tailored to the leader's role, domain, and pace.

AICC is the laid back, well-paced, more sophisticated sibling of the AI Stakeholder Challenge (AISC). The sophistication is in the **mode of delivery** (private, conversational, boardroom-paced) and the **depth** (counsel that goes as deep as the leader's mandate requires). It is built for C-suite executives and important personalities who cannot, or would rather not, do the intense 7-day challenge.

Core premise, shared with AISC: every leader is tasked with DIRECTION (where the organisation or domain goes), PEOPLE (who does the work and how they grow), and EXECUTION (how things get done). AI now cuts across all three. The scarce resource is judgment. AICC develops that judgment privately, at the leader's pace.

## The Three Emphases

AICC carries the AISC pillars, but weighted differently. This weighting is the product's identity — never flatten it back to the AISC balance.

| Emphasis | What it means in AICC |
|---|---|
| **AI Clarity** | The primary promise. The knowledge layer of AI Fluency: an honest, unbiased picture of what AI actually is, built by the leader themselves, not borrowed from a vendor pitch or a consultant's slide. Fluency is always anchored by the four Es: interacting with AI in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical, and safe**. In AICC, clarity is delivered conversationally, session by session, in the leader's own context. |
| **AI Leadership** | Central to AICC. How to direct, delegate, and govern in an AI environment: leadership alignment, risk oversight, governance mandates, and the direction a Chief hands down to the people who execute. A Chief leaves AICC as the person their world turns to for AI direction. |
| **AI Value** | Understanding how to **extract and exploit value** from AI in the leader's domain. AICC is NOT territory-hunting: there is no requirement to find and declare a territory (that is AISC's capstone). The AICC leader learns where value lives, how to capture it, and how to judge the AI investments and proposals that cross their desk. |

**Labs are light.** Hands-on AI environments appear only as far as they serve clarity, and go deeper only when the Chief is interested. Never sell AICC on hands-on intensity; sell it on clarity, counsel, and command.

## Who It Is For: Chiefs and VIPs

AICC uses the same three qualifiers as AISC — the title matters less than the reality:

- **Responsibility** — initiative and accountability. You own outcomes, not just tasks.
- **Influence** — people who listen to you. You shape direction for others.
- **Resources, or access to them** — the wherewithal to act on the highest level of value clarity provides.

A Chief is anyone with real decision authority, a following or team, and control of resources — capital, relationships, or platform. AICC serves the full breadth of that definition, not only corporations:

- Corporate executives (CEO, COO, CFO, CTO, CIO, CMO, CHRO, Founder, Board Member, Commissioner)
- Politicians and elected officials with budget and constituency authority
- Senior religious leaders: Bishops, Senior Pastors, Imams, General Overseers, Presiding Officers
- High-influence public figures: celebrities, entertainers, personalities with large, monetizable audiences
- Large business owners with significant staff and operational scale
- Institutional leaders: Vice Chancellors, Hospital Directors, NGO Executives, Military Commanders
- VIPs and important personalities whose position makes AI a question they are expected to answer

Their reality: they are being asked what the plan is — by their board, congregation, constituency, audience, or team. They have heard about AI from every direction. What they lack is a clear, personal framework built for someone at their level, delivered at their pace.

Their core desire: a mandate. Not more information — a decision. Where do I stand? What do I own? How do I lead this?

Eligibility: a minimum of 10 years of professional experience with strategic or P&L decision-making authority. Enrollment is limited to 5 seats per month.

## AICC and AISC: the relationship

- AISC is the intense 7-day community challenge; its capstone is a declared AI Value territory and a 6-Month Roadmap.
- AICC is the private, paced path: same clarity DNA, delivered 1:1 over four weeks, value framed as extraction rather than territory declaration.
- AISC's own brief says Chiefs "come primarily for AI Clarity and a conversational, directional experience — not community-based execution." AICC is that experience as a complete product.
- Never frame one as inferior. AISC is the challenge; AICC is the counsel. A Chief may do either or both.

## The Journey (four weeks, eight sessions)

Three arcs — Understand, Experiment, Govern — paced across four weeks. These are narrative arcs of one engagement, NOT separately purchasable phases. Phase-based enrollment is retired; never present the arcs with individual prices.

- **Understand** — the strategic briefing layer: jargon-free clarity on what AI is, what it means for the leader's domain, risk and opportunity mapping.
- **Experiment** — guided, private exposure to AI environments, calibrated to the leader's interest. A Personal Adoption Pilot tests AI on one real workflow.
- **Govern** — leadership alignment, governance and policy toolkit, value extraction strategy, and the direction the leader will hand down. Council engagements add group sessions here.

Scheduling runs on Executive Windows (e.g., Mon & Wed or Tue & Thu, with Saturday intensives available). Sessions are 60–90 minutes.

## What Participants Receive

- Tailored 1:1 strategic counsel for their specific role, industry, and priorities
- A Personal Adoption Pilot on a real workflow
- The proprietary Governance & Policy Toolkit ("Board-Ready" frameworks)
- A post-program Strategic Capstone option (2–3 months of guided application, six capstone tracks)

## The Council

The group engagement of three, for organizations and high-trust groups: an executive team, a founder and two lieutenants, a board faction, a family office, partners in a venture. Not "enterprise" — the word implies procurement and headcount; The Council implies alignment among people who already trust each other. At least 2 of the 3 at Executive/Leadership level. Combines group sessions (alignment) with individual sessions (personal clarity).

## What AICC Is NOT

- **Not a challenge.** No cohort, no community execution, no daily assignments. The pace is the point.
- **Not phase-purchasable.** One engagement, three offers. The arcs are narrative, not products.
- **Not a technology course.** Leaders direct — they don't build the tools.
- **Not a labs bootcamp.** Hands-on depth is available, not required.
- **Not territory-hunting.** Value in AICC means extraction and exploitation in the leader's existing domain; the declared-territory capstone belongs to AISC.
- **Not for the merely curious.** The 10-year eligibility line and 5-seats-per-month cap are filters, stated calmly as fact.
